# Manitoba Agricultural Data Portal: online demo

A public copy of the Phase 1 prototype interface. **All values, identifiers, connections and boundaries are fabricated demonstration data.** It is not an official or operational system.

This hosted copy uses pre-generated answers for five example questions. The full prototype also runs a local language model, which is not part of this site.

Live demo: https://digitalagroecosystemslab.github.io/manitoba-data-hub-demo/

## About

This prototype illustrates the proposed **Manitoba Soil Data & Intelligence Hub**, prepared to support discussion of a potential NSERC Alliance partnership. Soils are the proposed Phase 1 focus.

- **Prepared by:** Nasem Badreldin, [Digital AgroEcosystems Lab](https://github.com/DigitalAgroecosystemsLab), University of Manitoba.
- **University of Manitoba:** soil laboratory, mapping, spectroscopy and geospatial research are the starting point for the proposed Hub.
- **Government of Manitoba:** a potential partner. No funding or partnership commitments are implied, and the logos shown do not indicate endorsement of this demonstration.
